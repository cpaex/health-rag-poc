Patient: Tavares, Maria L    MRN-0010001    DOB 1958-03-14
Encounter date: 2025-04-18
Provider: Dr. H. Sandberg, Radiology

HPI:
62-year-old woman referred for CT angiography of the chest to evaluate exertional
dyspnea. She has essential hypertension and type 2 diabetes. During a prior
outpatient CT with iodinated contrast approximately one month ago she reportedly
"went blotchy and tight in the throat" a few minutes into the injection and the
study was paused. No prior formal allergy workup. No history of asthma. Takes
metformin and lisinopril.

Assessment:
Likely a prior hypersensitivity-type response to iodinated contrast media,
moderate severity (mucocutaneous plus subjective airway tightness, no documented
hypotension). Not a true anaphylaxis by the records available, but the reaction
pattern warrants caution.

Plan:
- Proceed with non-contrast CT today; defer contrast-enhanced imaging.
- If contrast study becomes necessary, use a low-osmolar agent with a
  corticosteroid and antihistamine premedication protocol, and observe for 30
  minutes post-injection.
- Document the reaction prominently in the chart.
- Endocrine and primary care to be notified.
